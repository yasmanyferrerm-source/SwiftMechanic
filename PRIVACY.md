# SwiftMechanic Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 5, 2026 — yasmanyfmSoft (yasmanyferrerm@gmail.com)_

**SwiftMechanic does not have its own servers and does not collect, store, sell or share your personal data.**

## Data stored on your device
Your vehicles, problem descriptions, photos you attach, diagnosis history, follow-up answers and your AI API key are saved only on your phone. Uninstalling the app, "Clear history" and "Remove vehicle" delete them.

## Data sent off your device
- **AI provider (optional).** Only if you add your own API key, the problem description, vehicle details (year, make, model, trim, engine, mileage, VIN if entered), scanner codes/live data and any photo you attach are sent over HTTPS to the provider you chose (Anthropic or Google) to generate the diagnosis. Their privacy policies apply: https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy and https://policies.google.com/privacy
- **NHTSA (U.S. government).** The vehicle's year, make and model are sent to NHTSA's public APIs to look up recalls and owner complaints; a VIN you enter is sent to NHTSA vPIC to decode it.
- **fueleconomy.gov.** Year, make and model are sent to list submodels and engines.

## Bluetooth and location
Bluetooth is used only to talk to an OBD-II adapter you choose. On Android 11 and older, Android requires location permission to scan for Bluetooth devices; SwiftMechanic never reads or stores your location.

## Children
SwiftMechanic is not directed at children under 13.
